ABOUT THE PROJECT

Clear learning over quick wins.

FX Learning Lab is an independent educational project exploring how currency markets work and what financial risk means in practice.

What we teach

We focus on clear market mechanics, illustrated examples, realistic execution costs, risk literacy and learning exercises. The beginner course has five lessons. More material may be added following editorial review.

Editorial policy

We distinguish established market mechanics from opinions and hypotheses. Numerical examples are labeled as hypothetical. We verify arithmetic, document sources, explain assumptions and correct identified errors. We do not sell signals or promise trading performance.

Foundational reading includes the author-developed Japanese manuscript FX取引分析大全 (2026), adapted into original English learning materials. Market and financial risk references include the Bank for International Settlements, the CFTC forex risk advisory and Investor.gov.

Risk disclosure

Foreign exchange and leveraged derivative products carry substantial risk and are not suitable for everyone. Losses may occur rapidly and, depending on the product and jurisdiction, may exceed deposits. Illustrative examples, quizzes and calculators are not personalized investment advice and should not be used as trade recommendations. Broker terms, protections, margin requirements and executions differ.

Funding and editorial independence

FX Learning Lab offers free educational materials. The site carries no broker promotion, sponsorships or advertising.

Accuracy and sources

Hypothetical examples are not live market quotes. Source links may describe regulatory treatment within specific countries and do not necessarily apply in your jurisdiction. Published pages are educational, not individualized advice.
